    System Architecture & Engineering

    Private Python/Keras Source
    Subsystem Design & Methodology
    Leak-Free Preprocessing Split-first chronological pipeline with TrainOnlyMinMaxScaler fitted strictly on training dates.
    Technical Indicators Stdlib Python RSI(14), EMA20/50, MACD(12,26,9), and OBV with zero post-warmup NaNs.
    Baseline Benchmarking Direct comparison against naive persistence (y_t = y_{t-1}) on the held-out split.
    Safe Serialization Static ZIP/JSON archive inspection blocking anonymous Lambda layers, paired with custom attention layers.
    Preview Boundary Uses only synthetic SYNTH-EQ data with no real market CSVs, model binaries, or training logs.
